About Us

Swap Skill is a skill-sharing and learning platform designed to connect people who want to learn and share their knowledge with others.

Our platform encourages users to exchange skills, discover new talents, and build meaningful learning connections in a supportive environment.

We believe that everyone has something valuable to teach and something new to learn.
